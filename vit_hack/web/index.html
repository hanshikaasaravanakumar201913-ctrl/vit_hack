Dosing schedule & exposure rules

Subjects are randomized 1:1 at Baseline to active investigational drug or matching placebo:

Active Drug Arm (DRUG)

Administered dose is strictly 10 mg per scheduled protocol visit (Baseline through Week 24).

Placebo Arm (PLACEBO)

Administered dose is strictly 0 mg per scheduled protocol visit.

Deviation criteria: Any subject receiving a dose not concordant with their randomized arm (e.g. 20 mg instead of 10 mg, or > 0 mg in placebo) is deterministically flagged as a dosing deviation. At Site S09, 6 subjects received 20 mg across multiple visits.

Liver safety (Hy's law) & serious adverse events

To detect drug-induced liver injury, ATLAS deterministically implements the FDA Hy's law guidance:

1 Transaminase Elevation: Serum ALT > 3× ULN (or AST > 3× ULN).
2 Hyperbilirubinemia: Total Bilirubin > 2× ULN.
3 Temporal Window: Bilirubin collected within 14 days of qualifying transaminase elevation.
Serious Adverse Events (SAE): Per protocol Section 6, any adverse event resulting in inpatient hospitalization (AESHOSP = Y) is classified as a Serious Adverse Event (SAE), regardless of the investigator's initial seriousness assessment.
